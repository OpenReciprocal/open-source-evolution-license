Section 5 Evolution

5.1 The step. A Recipient that distributes a Modified Version still under this License may evolve that Modified Version to an Evolution Target. The step is valid only when the Recipient meets every condition in this Section at the time of that distribution. Evolution is a power of the Recipient. This License does not require the Recipient to use it.

5.2 No step without a shipment, and no step for an ancestor. The Recipient must actually distribute the Modified Version. A statement that is not part of a distribution evolves nothing. Evolution has no effect on an Ancestor Version or on an unmodified copy. Those remain under this License.

5.3 The record. The distribution must include an Evolution Record. In a source tree, the record is a file named EVOLUTION.md in the root directory. If the form of distribution has no root directory, the record is a notice a recipient of that form would ordinarily see with the Software, containing the same items. The record contains:

(a) this statement, with the blanks filled: "Under Section 5 of the Open Source Evolution License, Version 0.2, <Recipient> distributes version <version> (<date>) under <Evolution Target name and version>. The ancestor version remains under OSEL-0.2.";

(b) the Evolution Target's unmodified text, or the address of the license steward's official copy;

(c) the Menu entries the target relies on, each tied to a provision of the target, sufficient to show Section 1.11(b) and (c);

(d) the name of the Recipient taking the step.

The Recipient does not delete or alter an Evolution Record that came with the Software. Section 5.7 says when no further Evolution under this License is available.

5.4 The line keeps its papers. The evolved distribution must still include this License and the copyright notices required by Section 2, so the ancestor terms remain readable.

5.5 The step binds the one who takes it. After a valid Evolution, that Recipient distributes that evolved version only under the Evolution Target, subject to Section 5.6. That Recipient does not distribute that evolved version again under this License or under a license with fewer duties than the Evolution Target.

5.6 What remains beside the target. The Evolution Target governs the evolved version. The following also remain:

(a) copyright notices and attribution, kept to the stricter of this License and the Evolution Target;

(b) Section 6 and Section 7, unless the Evolution Target provides a warranty disclaimer and a limitation of liability at least as protective of Contributors and Copyright Holders;

(c) Section 3, on the terms of Section 3.4.

5.7 Where this procedure stops. Once a version has validly evolved, further distribution of that version follows the Evolution Target. Section 5 does not authorize a second evolution of that version under this License. The Evolution Record stays with the distribution.

5.8 A failed step is not an Evolution. If a Recipient purports to evolve and any condition of this Section or of Section 1.11 is unmet, no Evolution occurs. The Modified Version remains under this License as against every Recipient who received it under this License. A Recipient who offers that Modified Version solely under the purported target, without a valid Evolution, breaches this License.

5.9 These are not Evolution Targets. A Recipient does not evolve to:

(a) a license that is not an OSI-Approved License;

(b) a license that fails Section 1.11, including a license whose duties are fewer than or equal to the duties of this License;

(c) a license that adds a restriction on a field of endeavor, a person, a group, or a business model.
